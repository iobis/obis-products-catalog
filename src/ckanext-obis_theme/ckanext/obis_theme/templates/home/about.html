    {# ---- Community ---- #}

    {{ _('Community effort') }}

    {% trans pcg_link='https://manual.obis.org/nodes.html#obis-products-coordination-group', helpdesk='mailto:helpdesk@obis.org' %} This catalogue is developed through the OBIS Products Coordination Group (PCG). The PCG is tasked with coordinating data and information products, from any type of analysis that synthesises and generates new information from data hosted on OBIS and other sources. The PCG meets every other month and is open to attendance by the OBIS community. More information can be found in the OBIS Manual, and by contacting the OBIS Helpdesk (helpdesk@obis.org). {% endtrans %}
